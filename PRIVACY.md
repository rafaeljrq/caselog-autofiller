PRIVACY POLICY — Case Log Autofiller

Last updated: [9/2/26]

WHAT THIS EXTENSION DOES WITH YOUR DATA

- Nothing leaves your computer. This extension does not have a server. It does not
transmit, collect, sell, or share any data with the developer or any third party.
It contains no analytics and no tracking.

DO NOT PASTE PATIENT IDENTIFIERS

- Before pasting a note into the extension, remove names, medical record numbers,
dates of birth, and any other information that identifies a patient. The fields
the extension reads (age range, ASA class, anesthetic type, airway technique,
nerve blocks, procedure category) do not require identifiers. The extension does
not look for or extract medical record numbers. If a paste appears to contain an
identifier label, the extension shows a warning asking you to remove it.

WHAT IS STORED, AND WHERE

- Stored locally in your browser's extension storage, on your device only:
- The case entries you add to your queue (extracted fields such as ASA class,
  anesthesia type, and airway technique)
- Your program configuration (site list, attending roster, vocabulary,
  preferences)

The original text of any note you paste is never stored.

You can delete all stored data at any time by removing the extension from your
browser, or by clearing the queue and resetting setup within the extension.

YOUR RESPONSIBILITY

- Notes you work with may contain protected health information. Everything the
extension processes stays on your own device and is never transmitted, but you
remain responsible for complying with your institution's policies and applicable
law regarding where and how you handle patient information.

THIRD PARTIES

- None. No data is shared with anyone.
